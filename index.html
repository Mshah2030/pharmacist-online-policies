Privacy Policy — Pharmacist Online Video Upload

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who we are. "Pharmacist Online Video Upload" is a private tool used only by Pharmacist Online (the YouTube channel and online shop run by Dr. Matahir Shah) to publish its own videos to its own Facebook Pages (Pharmacist Online, Alt Meds and Ancient Persia), Instagram accounts and Threads profiles.

Who can use it. Only our own team. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data it uses.

  • Our own videos, captions and post times, which we create.
  • Basic information about our own Pages and accounts (name, ID) so posts go to the right place.
  • Results of our own posts (views, likes, comments counts and insights) so we can see what works and avoid posting the same video twice.

What it does not do. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about the people who watch, like or comment on our posts. It does not send messages to anyone. It does not use data for advertising.

How data is kept. Access keys are kept only in locked secret storage (GitHub Actions secrets / Google Cloud Secret Manager), never in files or chats. Post records (post IDs, times, counts) are kept in our private Google Cloud storage for up to 24 months, then deleted.

Deleting data

To ask us to delete any data related to you, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days. You can also remove this app's access at any time in your Facebook settings under "Apps and websites".

Health information. Our posts are general education only, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist.
